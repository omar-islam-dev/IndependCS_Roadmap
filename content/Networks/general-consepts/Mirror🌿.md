**Mirror** is a replica (copy) of a site or a server which is often located in a different geographic region than the original
mainly for reducing network traffic, improving access speed, ensure availability of the original site for technical or political reasons, or providing a real-time backup of the original site
the concept of mirroring applies to network services accessible through any protocol, such as HTTP or [[FTP🌿]]
