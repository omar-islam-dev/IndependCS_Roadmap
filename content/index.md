## 🧭 How to Navigate

This garden is non-linear. You can explore by following **Wikilinks** (the highlighted text) to jump between related concepts, or use the **Graph View** on the right to see how topics connect.

### 🏷️ Content Maturity (The Garden Metaphor)
Not all notes here are finished products. To help you manage expectations, I tag my notes with their current growth stage:

> [!note] Note Status Guide
 >  - 🌱 **Seedling:** A rough idea or a quick scratchpad. Likely incomplete or unpolished.
 >  - 🌿 **Budding:** A topic I am actively working on. It has structure but is still growing.
 > - 🌲 **Evergreen:** Mature content. Well-documented, reviewed, and stable. Reference-quality.
